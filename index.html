Built for people who have never played

Most chess sites drop you straight into a board and expect you to know what a bishop is. This one starts from "this is a board, these are pieces".

Learn tab — 11 interactive lessons

Board, pawn, knight, bishop, rook, queen, king, check & checkmate, back-rank mate, castling, stalemate. Every lesson is a sandbox: click or drag the pieces and the app talks back. You cannot break anything.

Guided first game

Press "Start guided first game" and the coach tells you exactly what to play — e4, knight, bishop, castle — and explains in plain English what the computer is doing back to you.

“Why?” on every move

Stuck mid-game? One button names the best move and gives the reason in plain words, then draws a blue arrow on the board. Unlimited undo is always available — it is a training tool, not cheating.

An AI that starts easy

Six levels from Friendly (brand new) — rated around 800, makes real beginner mistakes so you can actually win your first games — up to ~1900. Runs entirely offline in your browser.

343 tactics puzzles

Mate-in-1, mate-in-2, "find the tactic" and only-move defence puzzles. In beginner mode they are sorted easiest-first so you do not get crushed on puzzle one.

Blunder Guard

A safety net that warns you before you throw away material, reports on your mistakes after the game, and shows what the computer was threatening.

The 7-day crash course

Each day is 60–90 minutes: a short lesson, a drill, and games. Progress, XP and ranks are saved in your browser.

DAY 1Board, pieces, legal moves & the one golden rule
DAY 2Check, checkmate & the back-rank mate
DAY 3Material, captures & the 6-question blunder firewall
DAY 4Openings & your repertoire (a plan, not memorisation)
DAY 5Endgames that win points: K+Q, K+R, K+P
DAY 6Tactics blitz — forks, pins, skewers, discovered attacks
DAY 7Traps, time management & match-day routine

How to start

Three steps, about two minutes.

1

Open the trainer

Press Start training. It runs in the page — nothing to install, no account, no network needed once it is loaded.

2

Answer one question

"Where are you starting from?" Pick I have never played and the app turns on Beginner Mode and opens Lesson 1 for you.

3

Lesson, then guided game, then Day 1

Drag pieces in the lessons, play the guided first game with the coach talking you through it, then switch the top-bar pill to Training Mode and start the 7-day plan.
